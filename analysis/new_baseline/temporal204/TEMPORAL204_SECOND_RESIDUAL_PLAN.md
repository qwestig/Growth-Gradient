## Method

First-layer OOF prediction:

```text
pred_210_oof = temporal204_base * exp(global_centered_GRU_oof_log)
```

Second-layer target:

```text
target_2 = log(actual / pred_210_oof)
```

Second-layer model:

- CatBoostRegressor
- depth `3`
- `l2_leaf_reg=80`
- `iterations=300`
- small clipped gamma at submission time

This is closer to safe residual stacking than literal GRU boosting.
